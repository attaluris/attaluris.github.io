Triad

Every card has four features: number, color, shape and fill. Three cards form a triad when each feature is either the same on all three or different on all three. If two cards share something the third doesn't, it's not a triad.

Options and two-player rules
  • When you see a triad, call it: press your key or your Call button. You then have 6 seconds to tap the three cards.
  • A correct triad goes to your pile for +1. A wrong pick or running out of time costs 1 point.
  • When the table has no triad, three more cards are dealt. If you're both stuck, press Deal 3 more (D). If a triad was there, it's shown before the new cards come out. The game ends when the deck is empty and no triads remain.
